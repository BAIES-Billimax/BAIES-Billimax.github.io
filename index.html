Billimax asksWhat should happen next—and why?

One patient context across the visit and revenue cycle.

Billimax is intended to connect authorized practice, clinical, patient, insurance and financial data into a common timeline so staff can move from exception to explanation to action.

01

Patient Intake & Interactive Visit Discovery

Prepare forms and signatures, combine current responses with authorized prior history, and surface relevant follow-up questions or potential treatment/procedural opportunities for staff or clinician review.

02

Patient Opportunity Engine

Identify recall, existing or unscheduled treatment, scheduling and insurance-timing opportunities, then support appropriate office-approved outreach by text, email or other channels.

03

Pre-Walkout / Pre-Claim Reconciliation

Identify missing or inconsistent procedures, documentation, coding, coverage, scheduling, attachments and payer-required information before the patient leaves and before claim submission.

04

Revenue Recovery & Collections

Track eligibility, benefits, claim status, payer response, denials, underpayments, A/R follow-up and patient responsibility after adjudication.

05

Finance, Audit & Practice Intelligence

Reconcile financial and operational activity, monitor production, collections, payer performance and A/R, identify exceptions or missed charges, and support financial, auditing and practice-management reporting.

Built as an intelligence layer over the systems a practice already uses.

Billimax is intended to use vendor-supported integration methods and authorized data access. Practice-management facts, payer-connected data and Billimax recommendations remain distinguishable and traceable.

Security and authorization are part of the product design.

Development is intended to begin with supported, minimum-necessary access and clear separation between testing and authorized production data.

Read-only firstNo production write operations without vendor and dental-practice approval.
Vendor-supported integrationUse supported APIs and partner pathways rather than unauthorized direct database access.
Minimum necessaryLeast-privilege access, secure credential management, tenant isolation and PHI-safe logging.
Source provenancePractice facts, payer responses and Billimax-derived recommendations remain distinguishable.
