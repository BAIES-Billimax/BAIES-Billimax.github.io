Practice intelligence for dental teams

AI for the modern dental practice.

BAIES develops Billimax, an AI-powered dental practice operations and revenue-cycle platform. Billimax supports patient intake and interactive visit discovery, patient opportunity identification, pre-walkout/pre-claim reconciliation, revenue recovery and collections, and finance, audit, and practice intelligence. It is designed to become increasingly tailored to each participating practice by using that office’s authorized historical data, workflows, payer mix, and operating patterns to provide context-specific assistance and recommendations.
